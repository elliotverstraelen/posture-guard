# PostureGuard — Demo Video Script

**Target length:** ~2–3 minutes  
**Format:** Screen recording of the running app + narration  
**Tone:** Clear, confident, first-person walkthrough

---

## [0:00 – 0:20] Hook

> "Most people spend 6 to 8 hours a day sitting at a desk — and most of that time in poor posture without even realising it. PostureGuard is a macOS menu bar app that watches your camera in real time and nudges you before your back gives out."

*Show: menu bar with the 🧍 icon. Zoom in slightly.*

---

## [0:20 – 0:50] Calibration

> "The first thing you do is calibrate. Sit in the position you'd normally work in — not artificially straight, just your natural working posture — and click Calibrate."

*Click the menu bar icon → click "🎯 Calibrate now".*

> "PostureGuard records 60 frames of your body landmarks from the camera and averages them into a personal baseline. All alerts from now on are relative to YOUR posture, not some hardcoded average. This takes about two seconds."

*Show the 📐 calibrating icon in the menu bar, then the notification "Calibration complete ✓ — Baseline saved."*

> "Once it's done, the icon turns green — we're monitoring."

---

## [1:00 – 1:40] Bad posture → notification

> "Now let me show what happens when I slouch."

*Visibly slouch forward toward the camera — hunch shoulders, drop head.*

> "The score drops immediately — you can see it going from green to yellow to red in the menu bar. If I hold this bad posture for 30 seconds, the app fires a notification."

*Wait a moment — notification appears. Open it.*

> "The notification includes a specific coaching tip — in this case the tip was generated by a local Ollama language model that I had running. It knows which posture signals fired and gives personalised advice."

*Read the tip aloud.*

> "When I sit back up, I get a 'posture restored' confirmation."

*Sit upright — show "🟢 Good posture restored" notification.*

---

## [1:40 – 2:10] Live View

> "I can also open the Live View, which shows all my cameras simultaneously with the MediaPipe skeleton overlay and the real-time score for each feed."

*Click "📷 Live View" from the menu.*

*Show the dual-camera window with green dots on face/shoulders and the triangle connecting nose + shoulders.*

> "This is useful for understanding exactly what the system sees. Each dot is one of 33 body landmarks tracked by MediaPipe Pose. The score shown here is calculated live from the geometric relationships between those points."

---

## [2:10 – 2:35] Settings

> "In Settings I can switch between cameras, enable phone detection — which alerts me if I've been looking down at my phone for more than 10 minutes — and tune the alert timing."

*Open ⚙️ Settings. Point to camera picker, phone detection toggle, alert delay field.*

> "The Calibration Studio section lets me label saved pose snapshots as good or bad. PostureGuard automatically captures snapshots of sustained postures — ones I've held for at least 15 seconds. Over time, labeling these lets me personalise the detection thresholds."

*Point to the label count ("0 of 10 labeled — 10 more labels needed").*

---

## [2:35 – 3:00] Close

> "PostureGuard runs entirely locally — no data ever leaves the machine. It uses MediaPipe for real-time pose estimation, Ollama for AI coaching tips, and HuggingFace for session-level analysis. It installs in one command via Homebrew and sits quietly in the menu bar, out of the way until you need it."

*Show the menu bar icon one more time — score 🟢 92.*

> "Thanks for watching."
